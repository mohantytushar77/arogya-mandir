About Us

Where Ancient Care Meets Modern Medicine

Arogya Mandir (ଆରୋଗ୍ୟ ମନ୍ଦିର), meaning "Temple of Health," operates in partnership with Maa Sarala Medicine Store near the Bus Stand in Nilgiri, Balasore, Odisha. We believe quality healthcare should be accessible to every community.

Led by Dr. Nirmalya Mohapatra, an AIIMS New Delhi–trained specialist in Pain Medicine & Rehabilitation, we provide comprehensive non-surgical treatment for orthopedic, neurological, rheumatological, and chronic pain conditions.

Our Services
Comprehensive Pain & Orthopedic Care
Advanced non-surgical treatments tailored to restore your mobility and eliminate chronic pain.
🦴

Orthopedic Treatment

Complete care for fractures, joint disorders, sports injuries, and musculoskeletal conditions using advanced non-surgical techniques.

⚡

Pain Management

Specialized interventional pain procedures including nerve blocks, trigger point injections, and PRP therapy for chronic pain relief.

🧠

Neurological Rehabilitation

Expert rehabilitation for stroke recovery, nerve injuries, neuropathies, and other neurological conditions affecting movement.

🔬

Rheumatological Care

Treatment for arthritis, autoimmune disorders, fibromyalgia, gout, and other rheumatic conditions with a holistic approach.

🏋️

Physiotherapy & Rehab

Personalized rehabilitation programs combining manual therapy, electrotherapy, and therapeutic exercises for optimal recovery.

💉

Joint & Spine Injections

Precision-guided intra-articular injections, epidural steroid injections, and facet joint blocks for targeted pain relief.
